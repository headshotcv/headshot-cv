---
name: HeadshotCV demo-video creative direction
description: The required look/feel for the demo-video artifact
---

The `demo-video` artifact (vertical 9:16 silent loop) must feel like an authentic, organic TikTok/UGC iOS **screen recording** of the real HeadshotCV app being filled out on a phone, ending on a finished CV.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected an earlier abstract animated/motion-graphics version as "too AI". They sent a reference WhatsApp video and want realism, not slick animation.

**How to apply:** Keep the whole 9:16 frame = the phone screen (no bezel). Use real-looking iOS chrome (Photos app for the CV hook, Safari for form steps), an AZERTY iOS keyboard with letter-by-letter typing, serif question titles, and end on the real CV. Avoid abstract animated scenes.
